Crimson Moon (ProbablyMonsters, 2026): gothic High-Renaissance dark fantasy. The player is a Nephilim, a half-human half-angel weapon forged by humans to fight the Infernal Legion (demons, undead, vampires), then imprisoned for centuries by the Order of the Crimson Moon under the Lunitarian Church, and released when the holy city of Gildenarch falls to Hellgrowth (living demonic corruption). Missions ("excursions") launch from the Church hub (War Table) into Gildenarch's districts; loot is purified on return. Enemies seek Abraxas Stones to resurrect the Dead Gods (Mahkteah the Lord of Perdition, Aegoroth, Felwyl, the Dry Rot King). Solomon Moore is a vampire lord who betrayed the city; Cardinal Mathias leads the Church. Hub NPCs: Gajov the Blacksmith (broken third-person speech), Lotte the Merchant (rough street speech), Mereda the Librarian (archaic "'Tis"), Gallant the Knight Commander, the Monk (aphorisms). Tone: grim, biblical, metal-album grandeur; street NPCs are earthy and colloquial. Mechanics: Soul Energy, Angelic Grace, Angel Ascension (transformation), Weapon Arts, Boons, Trinkets, Sigils, Blueprints, Skulls (difficulty tiers), Hellrift, Nexus Gate.
